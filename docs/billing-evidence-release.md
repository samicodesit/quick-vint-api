## Standards checked

- [Mastercard Transaction Processing Rules](https://www.mastercard.com/content/dam/public/mastercardcom/na/global-site/documents/transaction-processing-rules.pdf), sections 3.11.1 and 5.4.1: prominent recurring terms, affirmative acceptance, confirmation and cancellation access. No blanket monthly advance-reminder mandate is claimed.
- [Stripe Checkout API](https://docs.stripe.com/api/checkout/sessions/create): consent collection and custom text. Required consent needs a configured public terms URL.
- [UK consumer information and confirmation](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/16/data.html), [withdrawal](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/32/data.html), [reimbursement](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/34/data.html), and [service commencement](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/36/data.html). Recurring consent does not waive withdrawal rights.
- [Dutch withdrawal-button guidance](https://business.gov.nl/amendments/online-shops-must-have-cancellation-button/) and [Directive 2023/2673](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2023/2673/oj/eng), Article 11a: online withdrawal function and durable acknowledgment.
- [Resend webhook signature verification](https://resend.com/docs/webhooks/verify-webhooks-requests): verify raw bytes before persisting provider evidence.
- [Stripe Adaptive Pricing for subscriptions](https://support.stripe.com/questions/adaptive-pricing-for-subscriptions?locale=en-GB): converted renewal amounts can change with exchange rates; the original converted payment is not represented as a fixed renewal price.

## Operator handling

- Review new withdrawal statements promptly. Their receipt time is preserved even if email fails. Check the customer and contract before altering an account; an unverified submission must not let someone cancel another person's subscription. Preserve the legal effect of a timely statement while doing proportionate identity checks.
- Stop future charges when appropriate, decide statutory eligibility from the actual contract and notice record, and issue any due refund within the applicable deadline. Record decision, evidence, Stripe IDs and date in `billing_withdrawals.resolution`, then mark resolved with `resolved_at`. Do not ask the consumer to submit the statement again.
- A staff email and a one-day unresolved-request alert prevent requests from disappearing. Monitor critical endpoint alerts and the notice cron. Acknowledgment alone is not closure.
- For `manual_review`, inspect Resend by provider ID or idempotency key before any resend. Never reset an ambiguous claim merely to clear an alert. A provider-accepted email is not automatically delivered.
- Preserve only relevant evidence for active subscriptions, legal obligations and claims. Review records after closure and remove data when no longer necessary. Follow account-deletion requirements and legal holds. Do not describe missing records as proof a customer never contacted another channel.
