# Honest safety copy cleanup

## Goal

Describe AutoLister accurately as an AI listing assistant without absolute account, platform-policy, detection, or financial-safety promises.

## Public copy

- Remove the competitor-comparison section completely.
- Replace useful absolute claims with short factual wording: AutoLister prepares listing drafts, the seller reviews them, and the seller stays in control.
- Delete claims that add no useful product information instead of replacing them one-for-one.
- Keep ordinary legal-page phrases such as “Terms of Service”; they are not safety claims.

## Localization

Apply the same meaning across English, French, German, Dutch, Polish, Spanish, Italian, and Portuguese. Translations should sound natural rather than mirror English word-for-word.

## Scope

Update public website and extension-facing customer copy. Do not change listing behavior, legal documents, or product functionality.

## Validation

Search built public sources for the removed promises and competitor names, then run the existing website and extension build/test commands affected by the edits.
