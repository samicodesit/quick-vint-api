If you want to sell faster on Vinted, make it easy for someone to understand what they are buying. A clear first photo, useful details and a fair price give an interested buyer more confidence to take the next step.

You don't need to redo your whole wardrobe today. Pick a few items and make each one easier to understand.

## Give your first photo a job

Show the whole item clearly, with enough light to see its colour and shape. If a dress or pair of trousers is difficult to picture flat, a photo showing how it looks when worn can help.

A seller close to us tried this with her wardrobe. Her beige dress was listed with a model photo and went on to sell. The listing showed 135 views and 33 favourites.

## Show the details buyers ask about

Include the back, size label, fabric or texture, and any marks or wear. Measurements can help when fit matters. Showing a small flaw honestly helps the right buyer decide and avoids an awkward surprise later.

## Write a title that describes the item

Compare **Lovely dress** with **Beige long-sleeve mini dress with lace-up back**. Add a brand or size when you know it, and keep the description faithful to the item. Our [description guide](/blog/how-to-write-vinted-descriptions-that-sell) has more examples.

## Check similar items before setting a price

Look for comparable items in a similar brand, condition and size. Asking prices are a useful starting point, though they do not tell you what every item eventually sold for. Consider what you are happy to accept, and remember buyers see their total cost as well as the item price.

## Make a small batch easier

Choose three items and photograph them together. Then write their titles and descriptions while the details are fresh. AutoLister can help turn item photos and notes into editable listing drafts. Check the text, size, condition and photos before publishing.

## Before you publish

- Can a buyer see the whole item in the first photo?
- Have you shown labels, details and any flaws?
- Does the title describe the actual item?
- Have you checked similar listings and your price?
- Have you reviewed the draft and selected the correct size and condition?

Good photos and clear information give people more to work with. Start with one item you like and make it easy for someone else to see why.
