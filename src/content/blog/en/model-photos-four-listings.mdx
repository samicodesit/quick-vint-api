## A look at her Vinted balance

This screenshot shows her Vinted balance: €428.36 available and €574.20 pending. Seeing the sales come in was a lovely moment, and it made all those photos and listings feel worthwhile.

A piece of clothing can look very different when you can see its shape. My girlfriend wanted to help buyers picture her clothes being worn, so we added AI model photos alongside her original item photos. I build AutoLister, and this is the story of two items from her wardrobe.

## Try it with your own wardrobe

Start with an item whose shape is hard to see on a hanger or bed. Photograph the whole item, then include the back, label, fabric and any flaws. Keep the real details clear and review generated photos before using them. Add a specific title and honest description.
